The WAVES conference series

The biennial "International Conference on Mathematical and Numerical Aspects of Wave Propagation" (informally known as the "WAVES" conference) is the largest and longest-running international conference in the area of mathematical wave propagation.

Past WAVES conferences:

  • 1st Strasbourg, France, 1991
  • 2nd University of Delaware, United States, 1993
  • 3rd Mandelieu-La Napoule, France, 1995
  • 4th Golden (CO), United States, 1998
  • 5th Santiago de Compostela, Spain, 2000
  • 6th Jyväskylä, Finland, 2003
  • 7th Providence (RI), United States, 2005
  • 8th Reading, United Kingdom, 2007
  • 9th Pau, France, 2009
  • 10th Vancouver, Canada, 2011
  • 11th Gammarth, Tunisia, 2013
  • 12th Karlsruhe, Germany, 2015
  • 13th Minneapolis (MN), United States, 2017
  • 14th Vienna, Austria, 2019
  • 15th Palaiseau, France, 2022
  • 16th Berlin, Germany, 2024
  • 17th Montreal, Canada, 2026
