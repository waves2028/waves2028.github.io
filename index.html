Welcome to WAVES 2028

Welcome to the official website of WAVES 2028, the 18th International Conference on Mathematical and Numerical Aspects of Wave Propagation. This conference will bring together researchers from multiple disciplines, united by a common interest in the application of mathematical techniques to wave propagation problems.

The conference will be held on 26-30 June 2028 at the University of Bath, UK.

Further details about the conference will be announced in due course.
